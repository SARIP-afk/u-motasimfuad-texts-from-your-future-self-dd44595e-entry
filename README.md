A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one funny text message from
your future self to `/output/entry.txt` — the single text your future self
sends back to you.

## The text

> Future you here. That free trial ends tomorrow. In 2031 you're still paying $14.99 a month for it. You've never opened the app. Not once.

137 characters, 137 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this text wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: a funny text from the future self;
a real joke with a twist that pays off; a specific future-self voice; every
character used well; a fresh angle.

- **Future self, unmistakably.** "Future you here" opens it, and the whole
  message is a warning sent back to the present self.
- **A real joke with a double twist.** You are still paying for the trial
  years later — and you have never once opened the app. The last two words,
  "Not once.", land the punchline.
- **Specific, not generic.** $14.99 a month, the year 2031, "that free
  trial" — details that could only come from this person's life, not a
  stock joke.
- **Every character earns its place.** 137 characters, no wasted words, the
  punchline arriving in a short final sentence.
- **Fresh angle.** Not the obvious "still broke / still tired" joke, not a
  plant, not a haircut — the subscription trap everyone recognises but no
  one in this field wrote about.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes `out/entry.txt` with the text above.
